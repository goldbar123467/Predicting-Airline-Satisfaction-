## Promotion, robustness and v2 selection

Use the **existing second-pass policy**, recorded before these results, rather than inventing a significance claim:

1. For each completed candidate, report paired changes versus its matched control in pooled AUC, mean fold AUC, all three fold AUCs, selected lengths and wall time. A standalone improvement of at least 0.00005 in pooled and mean AUC, positive on at least two folds and with no fold below -0.00010, is useful evidence to prioritize follow-up. This is a practical information gate, not a p-value. Inferior standalone models may still pass the blend gate.
2. Compare only fixed-anchor mixtures `(1-alpha)*anchor + alpha*candidate` at the registered alpha grid **.05, .10, .20, .30**. Eligibility requires at least **0.00001 pooled gain**, **0.00001 mean-fold gain**, and **no fold regression worse than 0.00002**. These are tolerances for this campaign, not evidence that a ten-millionth or ten-thousandth change is statistically resolved. Log the full grid, including failures.
3. Keep the original v1 component weights and existing fixed seed groups locked. Add at most **three** new accepted candidates/groups by the registered constrained procedure. At each addition enforce the gate relative to the current anchored blend, and finally enforce it against the original v1 anchor too. Do not reopen zero-weight v1 candidates as free parameters or independently tune the members of a seed pair.
4. Slot 12 is permitted only when a new candidate has a fixed-anchor pooled and mean gain of at least **0.00003**, with all three fold changes nonnegative at the same alpha. Predeclare the seed before fitting; assess the equal average, not whichever seed is larger. The current trainer couples the seed to the inner stopping split, so this estimates training-plus-stopping variability. Do not repeat until one seed wins. Require the equal-average group to pass the ordinary blend gate before using it.
5. Preserve all three folds in the final report. Inspect candidate mean probabilities, log loss/Brier and fold AUC gaps only on development if there is fresh scale-drift evidence; do not fit per-fold transformations on outer OOF labels. An outer-fold leave-one-out weight-fitting stress test can be diagnostic, but is **not fully nested validation**: other OOF models may have trained on the held fold's labels. Do not label it a fresh test or use it to claim unbiased gains.

If no candidate clears the registered gate, v2 should retain the exact v1 blend and report the negative campaign. The operationally strongest deliverable may be unchanged predictions with better verification. If some pass, publish v2 as a **development-selected extension of v1**, with its own hashes and the complete experiment ledger. No new unbiased audit score is available. A paired development bootstrap, if reported, is conditional on already-selected models/weights and does not capture adaptive recipe selection or retraining variation; it must not be presented as a confidence bound on the entire campaign's improvement.

## Pipeline checks and known limits

- Every real-data smoke must join frozen splits and filter `fold >= 0` **before sampling**, preprocessing, model fitting or validation. Synthetic fixtures remain preferable. Enforce the check in executable boundaries; comments alone failed in the earlier campaign.
- Explicitly retain IDs, target encoding 0/1, positive-class column, and source hash/seed/environment provenance. Reuse the existing Python 3.12, torch 2.14.1+cu130, PyTabKit 1.7.3, CatBoost 1.2.10, LightGBM 4.7.0 and XGBoost 3.4.1 environment. No dependency upgrades are needed for these slots.
- Numeric profiles fit on the supplied training rows only. They use 16 actual numerical measurements, not arbitrary numeric encodings of categorical strings. Training rows may contribute their own **features**, but never labels; this is not target leakage, although rare-group training/inference distributions can differ. Unknown routes use train-only fallback means.
- TE keeps inner shuffled KFold independent of y, train-only priors/maps and frozen smoothing. Unknown keys map to the training prior. During development selection, neither audit nor test may fit dictionaries, profiles, encodings or neural normalization. A post-freeze full-data production refit may fit these transformations on all supplied training rows; test features still cannot fit them.
- The same model seed is necessary for a matched comparison but not a guarantee of bitwise equivalence. Changed dimensions can change initialization streams; GPU arithmetic and stopping variability remain. Compare paired results, record this limit and avoid causal claims about one numerical mechanism beyond the changed configuration factor.
- OOF routes each row to one held-out fold model; the fallback averages fold models on every test row, while the intended final release blends fixed-length full-data refits. Neither pooled OOF nor macro-fold AUC directly scores either deployed function. v1's historical audit assessed its own released recipe; it cannot validate altered v2 weights, components or full-data refits.
- Current native neural artifacts support inference and completed-run recovery, not exact optimizer continuation. Do not resume changed hyperparameters from a previous checkpoint and describe it as a matched fresh run. Preserve incomplete runs separately; only complete ID-aligned three-fold candidates can enter blending.
- Before releasing v2, load every selected native checkpoint and persisted transform, exercise batch sizes 1 and a partial batch, verify CPU/CUDA tolerance where relevant, recompute the weighted blend independently and check exact sample-submission schema, row order, coverage, bounds and finite values. Do not overwrite v1 or upload/submit anything without separate authorization.

Do not kill a short neural run solely on its first-epoch AUC. When repeated matched changes yield no gate-passing blend contribution, stop that branch instead of widening the search because time remains.
