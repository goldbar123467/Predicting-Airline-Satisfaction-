## The rating-distribution hypothesis and exact feature contract

The source hypothesis is the observed-category probability and summed-log feature in [goodpjw2008's Part4 auxiliary-task notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/goodpjw2008/s6e10-auxiliary-task-features-lb-0-96129), inspected in `research/second_pass_competition.md`. Reuse the 13 already frozen original-only XGBoost rating classifiers. Bank manifest SHA256: `4c0adfc0b94c5811d801cf058459159db3835d6bb8a0aa3a2029facc9c4d971c`. Their 129,859 cleaned original rows, vocabularies, 400-tree models and source-overlap exclusions remain fixed. Each bank omits its own rating from its 20 input predictors and never consumes satisfaction. Do not refit, recalibrate, change original exclusions or learn missing-value frequencies from development/test data. Remaining source ancestry and domain-shift risks persist despite exact-overlap removal.

For saved bank j, let p_j(c | x_-j) be its class probability. Define one fixed block:

1. Match observed rating r_j against that bank's explicit saved class-value array. For finite integer supported values, **q_j = p_j(r_j | x_-j), unchanged from native prediction even when below 1e-6**. For missing, fractional or unsupported observed ratings, q_j = 1e-6. Upstream raw-schema rejection of invalid infinities still applies. No ordinal interpolation, class-index arithmetic or data-dependent fallback. Supported q is not clipped.
2. Append q_0 through q_12 in the saved rating order as float32 continuous columns, then **S = sum_j log(max(q_j, 1e-6))**, reduced in float64 before casting to float32. Only the log argument is floored. S is bounded by approximately [-179.60164, 0]. Existing 13 EV features and their computation stay unchanged. q and S do not become categorical twins. Freeze the final feature names/order and categorical indices in metadata.
3. **Baggage handling, bank index 10, has classes [1,2,3,4,5], unlike the other banks' [0,1,2,3,4,5]. Its observed zero must use the floor.** Never index column zero as rating zero for this bank. Record unsupported/missing counts and supported probabilities below the log floor separately, without selecting a new epsilon from these counts. Root fixed this contract before cache construction. The canonical CPU cache logged 3,045 supported q values below 1e-6; their q features remain unchanged. An earlier draft of this note incorrectly floored q itself and is superseded by this correction, not by a retrospective feature change.
4. The observed rating is used only for selecting the probability after prediction; it must not enter its own bank's input. Features from these externally fitted banks can be cached for all rows because no synthetic satisfaction labels or synthetic-fit statistics enter them. Main-model transformations, categorical vocabulary, TE and route profiles remain fold-local.

Information rationale: equal predicted means can conceal different conditional distributions. Probabilities [.5,0,.5] and [0,1,0] on [0,1,2] both have expectation 1, but radically different probability for an observed 1. Raw rating plus EV cannot recover that distribution shape. This is a mechanism, not a measured gain. Rare categories, original-domain miscalibration and synthetic shift can instead make q misleading; the summed log feature can overemphasize unsupported zeros and correlated terms.

The sum of overlapping conditional logs is a **composite conditional score**, not a normalized joint likelihood, anomaly probability or causal quantity. [Varin, Reid and Firth (2011), sections 1-2](https://www3.stat.sinica.edu.tw/statistica/oldpdf/A21n11.pdf) define products of conditional/marginal components and explain dependence and misspecification. The paper supports this interpretation, not a satisfaction-classification gain. [VIME (NeurIPS 2020), sections 4.1 and 5](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/7d97667a3e056acab9aaf653807b4a03-Paper.pdf) supplies broader precedent for feature-reconstruction information; its masked neural pretraining and semi-supervised experiments do not reproduce our bank recipe. Neither paper was experimentally reproduced here.

### What the epoch test means

The local raw+EV run chose [3,3,3] epochs from four, while its already completed second seed chose [4,3,4]. This neither proves convergence nor establishes undertraining. Our wrapper evaluates every epoch and selects its best mean-ensemble inner AUC; it does not terminate immediately on a decline. [Pinned PyTabKit 1.7.3 scheduling source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dholzmueller/pytabkit/v1.7.3/pytabkit/models/training/scheduling.py) sets progress to epoch/max_epochs. The `flat_anneal` LR is flat through 60% then cosine-decays; decay and dropout also depend on normalized progress. Thus 12 epochs changes the earlier update trajectory and regularization timing. The existing outer refit uses its selected epoch count as a new schedule horizon, rather than replaying the exact prefix of the longer inner schedule. Keep this behavior matched in both cloud recipes and report it as a pipeline limitation.

Do not change label smoothing, LR, architecture or schedule simultaneously, and do not call this a pure additional-updates experiment. [Holzmueller, Grinsztajn and Steinwart's RealMLP paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.04491v3), methods/regularization, motivates carefully specified pretrained defaults and training schedules. Our very short competition recipe differs substantially from that benchmark setting; the paper supplies no predicted gain from 4 to 12 epochs here.

At each stage, consider `p_new = (1-alpha)*p_current + alpha*p_candidate`, with alpha in **[.05,.10,.20,.30]** only. Positive weights sum to one. Require all of:

- Pooled and mean-fold AUC each improve **at least .00001** against the current accepted mixture.
- No individual fold loses more than **.00002** against that mixture.

- Valid native artifacts, source/config/input hashes, OOF identities and the required platform control are present. For C1 specifically, its standalone pooled **and** mean-fold gains against C0 must be nonnegative, and no paired fold may lose more than .00002. This prevents admission based solely on a fortuitous environment change.

Select the largest qualifying pooled improvement; ties within 1e-12 prefer lower alpha, then the fixed registration order. Each frozen-v2-anchored search admits at most **two selected new candidate units in its complete release**, including any fixed seed group as one unit. Do not compound this into two more additions to a previous v3 release: each new search starts with the unchanged v2 anchor and considers the eligible registered v3 pool. Every later report retains the fixed-v2 comparison. Already released snapshots remain immutable; do not rewrite a frozen recipe. A new candidate fit is not itself authorization to bypass the incremental upload gate.
